The 5-Step Roadmap

The method, in the order it is taught: structure, paper, present, administer, delegate the raise.

1Structure the Company

Before a dollar moves, the company itself has to be structured correctly — the right entity, the right paperwork, the right ownership picture. Boutique firms raise money for structured opportunities; an unstructured company is not one.

2Write the Private Placement Memorandum

The private placement memorandum is the document that describes the opportunity, the terms, and the risks in writing. It is the paper serious money reads before it moves.

3Build a Strong Pitch Deck

The deck is the story in slides: the company, the opportunity, the numbers, the ask. The memorandum is the legal paper; the deck is what gets the meeting.

4Work With a Transfer Agent

A transfer agent handles the investor documentation properly — moving the information, the money, and the paper between the company and the investor, and keeping the records straight.

5Boutique Firms Raise It — for ~1%

Boutique investment firms exist to raise capital for structured opportunities. They go in and raise the money for you — and the model prices that work at around 1% of what they raise, while they raise millions.

Our educational notes (not from the video): real-world private placements sit inside securities law — exemptions, investor standards, and filing duties vary by deal and by state.
